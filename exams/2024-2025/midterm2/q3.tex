\section*{Question 3}
The magnetic field component of an monochromatic electromagnetic wave propagating in free space is given by plane waves as
\begin{equation*}
\vec{B}(z,t) = B_0 \sin(kz - \omega t) \hat{y}.
\end{equation*}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(a)] What is the direction of propagation of the wave? --- \textit{0.1 pts} 
    \item[(b)] Find the electric field component of the wave. --- \textit{0.2 pts}
    \item[(c)] Find the Poynting vector of the wave. --- \textit{0.2 pts}
    \item[(d)] How much power is carried by the wave through a circular area of radius \(a\) in the plane perpendicular to the direction of propagation? --- \textit{0.2 pts}
    \item[(e)] What is the  direction of polarization of the wave? --- \textit{0.2 pts} 
    \item[(f)] If the wave goes through a polarizer oriented at an angle $\pi/4$ with respect to the direction of polarization, what would be the amplitude of the transmitted wave? --- \textit{0.1 pts} 
\end{enumerate}
\noindent
Given: $\vec{B}_0 = \frac{1}{c} (\hat{z}\times \vec{E}_0)$ where $c$ is the speed of light in vacuum.
