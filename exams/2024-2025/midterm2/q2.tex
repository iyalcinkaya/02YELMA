\section*{Question 2}
A long solenoid, of radius $a$, is driven by an alternating current, so that the magnetic field inside the solenoid is sinusoidal:
\begin{equation*}
\vec{B}(t) = B_0 \sin(\omega t) \hat{z}
\end{equation*}
where $B_0$ is the amplitude of the magnetic field, $\omega$ is the angular frequency, and $\hat{z}$ is the unit vector in the direction of the solenoid's axis. A circular loop of radius $a/2$ and resistance $R$, is placed inside the solenoid, and is coaxial with it. 
\begin{enumerate}
  \item[(a)] Find the current induced in the loop as a function of time. --- \textit{0.5 pts}
  \item[(b)] Find the power dissipated in the loop as a function of time. --- \textit{0.5 pts}
\end{enumerate}
